Accessibility Walkthrough Checklist

Print one per showing. The homes that only satisfy 60–70% of this are your candidates for modification; 85%+ are move-in ready for your family.

Entry & Exterior

  • Zero-step or ramped main entrance
  • Attached garage with level entry to home
  • Wide driveway for van/lift access
  • Front walkway ≥ 36" wide, no broken pavers
  • Covered entry (snow/rain cover for mobility aid users)

Main-Level Bedrooms

  • Primary suite on main floor
  • At least one additional bedroom on main (for brother)
  • Bedroom doorways ≥ 32" clear (36" preferred)
  • Turnaround space (5'x5') beside bed
  • Closet rods reachable without overhead reach

Bathrooms

  • Curbless / roll-in shower on main level
  • Grab bars installed OR reinforced blocking present
  • Comfort-height toilet (17"–19")
  • Lever faucet handles (not knobs)
  • Non-slip flooring
  • Turn radius of 5' inside bathroom

Kitchen & Living

  • Open floor plan (kitchen–living–dining on one level)
  • Side-opening oven / wall oven
  • Counter section at 34" (or easily modified)
  • Pull-out shelves / drawer storage
  • Lever door handles throughout

Hallways & Flooring

  • Hallways ≥ 42" wide (48"+ ideal)
  • Minimal/no level changes on main floor
  • Hard-surface flooring (not plush carpet)
  • Main-level laundry room

Safety & Layout

  • Generator or solar backup (for medical equipment)
  • Bedroom near bathroom (night-time access)
  • Easy-to-reach thermostats & switches
  • Low-maintenance yard (or HOA-maintained)
  • Close to medical care (MS specialist: Univ. of Utah Health)

Multi-Gen Bonus

  • Separate entrance for brother's suite (privacy + caregiver access)
  • Main-floor den convertible to 2nd primary
  • Basement/upper level for guests, offices, or long-term care help

Worth asking a buyer's agent about

  • Has the agent worked with CAPS (Certified Aging-in-Place Specialist) remodelers?
  • Can they flag homes with reinforced bathroom blocking (common in newer Utah builds)?
  • Any 55+ communities you want to include (e.g., Sun City Mesquite alternatives in UT County)?
  • Proximity to University of Utah Health's MS Clinic — important if brother sees specialists there.
