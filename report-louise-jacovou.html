Interview Prep — Sparrow

1. "Walk me through how you manage a CEO's calendar when everything is a priority."
Talk points: your prioritization framework (impact vs. urgency), protecting deep-work/strategy blocks, sequencing meetings so the CEO is prepared, and how you say "no" or reschedule diplomatically. Reference your current Gen4 CEO calendar as proof.
2. "Tell me about a time you drafted communications in someone else's voice."
Talk points: a specific example of writing emails/updates as your executive, how you learned their tone, and the guardrails you use to stay accurate. Sparrow explicitly wants inbox ownership and voice-matching.
3. "How do you make sure CEO-dependent work doesn't fall through the cracks across the exec team?"
Talk points: your tracking system (action-item logs, follow-up cadence, status visibility to stakeholders), and an example where your follow-through unblocked a stalled initiative.
4. "This role flexes with little notice and includes some personal logistics. How do you feel about that?"
Talk points: show genuine comfort with ambiguity and the 90/10 EA/PA split; give an example of flexing your schedule for an urgent need while keeping boundaries healthy and sustainable.
5. "What process have you built that made an executive measurably more effective?"
Talk points: one concrete system (inbox triage rules, a weekly priorities ritual, a travel-prep template) and the time it saved. Sparrow screens for process-builders, not just task-doers.
